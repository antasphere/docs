Team repositories are real GitHub repositories, created from the event's starter with each member's GitHub account invited. `hackathon setup` clones your team's repository into a folder named after your team, and `hackathon doctor` tells you whether your machine runs the app and whether your push will go through, with the fix written for your operating system.
