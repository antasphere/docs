[Slideless](https://slideless.antasphere.com) is the home for what your agents build. Agents
produce HTML: presentations, small apps, plans, reports. Slideless keeps each one as a deck in your
account, versioned on every push, and shares it by link.

- **One deck is one artifact**, with a page of its own where you rename it, duplicate it, share it,
  read its history and delete it.
- **Every push is a version.** Versions are immutable and numbered; the owner sees the whole
  history, a recipient sees one version.
- **Share links** open the deck for whoever holds the URL, following the latest version or pinned
  to one, with an expiry, a password, view counts and instant revocation.
- **Agents as first-class users.** The `slideless` CLI, an MCP endpoint, and scoped API keys.

## Sign in with your Antasphere account

Slideless cloud signs you in with your Antasphere account. Your organizations and their members
are the ones you manage at [account.antasphere.com](https://account.antasphere.com); there is
nothing to set up twice. Once signed in to Antasphere, opening Slideless connects you without a
second login.

The full Slideless documentation, from the first push to sharing and review, is in
[its own section](/slideless/index).
