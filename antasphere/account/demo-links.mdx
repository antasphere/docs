A demo link signs one person in without their password. Whoever opens it in a browser is that
person, in the tool and on the page the link names. Demo links are made for demonstrations:
showing a tool as a member of an organization sees it, or handing a prepared account to a tester
for an afternoon.

## Not on your Antasphere account

Demo links are **off** on the Antasphere account, [account.antasphere.com](https://account.antasphere.com),
and they cannot be turned on there. Nobody can be signed in to a real Antasphere account through a
demo link.

They exist only on demonstration and test installations of Antasphere. On such an installation, a
banner on every page says that demo links are on, so that nobody works there without knowing it.

## Which accounts a link can open

A demo link only ever opens a **demonstration address**: an address on a domain reserved for
examples and tests, such as `ada@example.com` or `reviewer@acme.test`. An address that can receive
real mail, such as `ada@gmail.com`, is never opened by a demo link, whatever else is true.

A link also never opens:

- an account with a second factor (a link would walk past it);
- an account that has not confirmed its address;
- another owner's account;
- a person who also belongs to another organization: a demonstration account is one an owner
  invited into the organization for the demonstration, and it belongs nowhere else.

Only an owner of the organization, signed in with their password, makes a demo link. An API key
cannot make one.

## Where a link lands

A link names a tool and a page. Opening it signs the person in and takes them to that page of that
tool. A link that names no tool lands on the account's own dashboard.

The link decides where the person lands, not what they may do there: the tool's own access rules
apply to the person exactly as they would after a password sign-in. A person whose teams do not
have access to a tool is not let in by a link to it.

## One person per browser window

A link signs the whole browser in as its person: the account and every tool in that window are
that person from then on, and whoever the window was signed in as before is signed out of them.
There is no signing in as one person in one tab and another in the next. To show two people side
by side, open the second link in another browser profile or in a private window.

## How long a link lives

A day by default, a week at most: the owner chooses when making it, and can revoke it at any
moment. When a link expires or is revoked, the sessions it opened end with it: whoever is still
signed in through it is signed out.

A link is shown once, when it is made. Keep it like a password until it expires.

## Making links

On a demonstration installation, an owner makes demo links from the account settings or from the
command line with `antasphere demo link` (see [the CLI](/antasphere/agents/cli#demo-links)).
