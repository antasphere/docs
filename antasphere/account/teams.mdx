A team is a group of people inside one organization: the designers, the sales people, the members
working for one client. Teams let an organization give a tool, or a tool's projects, to some of its
people rather than to everyone.

## Who makes teams

Owners and admins of an organization create its teams on
[account.antasphere.com](https://account.antasphere.com), under **People > Teams**. There they name
a team, rename it, add and remove its members, and delete it. A team holds only members of its
organization, and one person can belong to several teams.

Deleting a team removes the group, not the people: its members stay in the organization with the
role they had.

## What teams are for

- **Opening a tool to some teams only.** An owner or admin can make a tool available to chosen
  teams of the organization instead of to every member. People outside those teams do not see the
  tool for that organization.
- **Sharing a tool's projects with a team.** A tool's projects can be shared with a team, and
  everyone in the team gets them.

Both are settled on the account site, never inside a tool: the tools read the teams from your
Antasphere account, so a change made once applies everywhere.

Some tools are **opened to an organization by Antasphere** rather than by its owners: an event's
tool, a tool run for one client. Such a tool appears in your organization's list only once
Antasphere has opened it there, everyone in the organization may then use it, and its card on
**Settings > Tools** says so without a setting to change. Ask Antasphere to open it or to change who
has it; an owner or admin cannot from the account site.

## What a member sees

Every member of an organization sees the teams they belong to on **People > Teams**. Inside each
tool, they see the teams that share its projects with them.

## When someone leaves

A person who leaves an organization, or is removed from it, leaves every team of that organization
at the same time. Their teams in other organizations are not affected.

## From the command line

The [`antasphere` CLI](/antasphere/agents/cli) works on the teams of the active organization:
`antasphere teams list` lists them, `antasphere teams members <team>` shows who is in one, and
`create`, `rename`, `delete`, `add <team> <email>` and `remove <team> <email>` change them. Reads
need a key with `account:read`; changes need a key with `account:write` held by an owner or an
admin of the organization (the key `antasphere login` stores carries both). With several organizations, add `--org <id>`, or run `antasphere org use <id>` once after `antasphere login` (a key set in the environment alone has no profile to remember the choice on).
