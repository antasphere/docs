One account for every Antasphere tool: sign in once at
[account.antasphere.com](https://account.antasphere.com) and you are connected everywhere. Every
tool in the family recognises the same identity, the same organizations and the same credentials,
so a login carries across the whole product family.

## Your account

- **Sign up in a minute.** An email address and a password, or a one-time code sent to your inbox.
  Verify the address once and the account is live.
- **A personal organization from day one.** Every account starts with an organization of its own.
  Create more for a team or a client, invite people by email, and give each member a role.
- **One place to manage it all.** Your profile, your organizations and their members, your API
  keys, two-factor authentication, and the audit trail of what happened on your account, from the
  dashboard at [account.antasphere.com](https://account.antasphere.com).
- **Your data stays yours.** Export everything an organization holds as a single archive, or delete
  the account outright, from the dashboard.

## The tools

Each tool signs you in with your Antasphere account ("Sign in with Antasphere") and reads your
organizations from it, so membership is managed once and applies everywhere.

- [**Slideless**](/antasphere/tools/slideless) — the home for what your agents build: HTML presentations,
  small apps, plans and reports, kept as versioned decks and shared by link.

## Agents and the command line

Agents are first-class users of your account. The [`antasphere` CLI](/antasphere/agents/cli) signs in with
a code sent to your email and manages your identity, organizations and files from the terminal;
[connecting an agent](/antasphere/agents/mcp-connector) pairs claude.ai, Claude Code or any MCP client with
your account in a few clicks.
