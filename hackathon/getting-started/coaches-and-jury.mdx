A coach helps every team from the Chats page; the jury reads the Teams page and scores the pitches on paper. Both sign in with their Antasphere account, on the email the organizers put on the roster.

## Coaches

The platform calls a coach an advisor. A coach has no team, no submission and no ballot, and reads what every
team reads and more:

- **Every team's chat**, on the **Chats** page, beside the general channel. Post in a team's chat like any
  member of it; the team, the other coaches and the organizers read it, no other team does.
- **Every team's help requests.** A participant opens a request for their team, a one-line title and what
  they need. Claim it, so the other coaches know it is taken, and answer in its thread. Let it go with an
  unclaim when someone else should take it. A member of the team resolves it when it is sorted; the thread
  then reads only, until someone reopens the request.
- **Every team's repository, read-only.** When the organizers put your GitHub username on the roster, GitHub
  invites you to read each team's repository. Accept the invitations (by email, or on github.com/notifications),
  then clone or pull any team's code to help them. You cannot push to it.
- **The Home page**, with the clock and the notices every participant sees, and the **Teams** page with each
  team's demo deck and presentation once they are handed in.

From the command line, after `npm i -g @antasphere/hackathon`, `hackathon login` and `hackathon connect`:

```bash
hackathon requests list --status open        # what waits for a coach
hackathon requests show <requestId>          # the request, its team, its thread
hackathon requests claim <requestId>         # take it
hackathon requests unclaim <requestId>       # let it go
hackathon chats list                         # every channel you can read
hackathon chats read <channelId>             # one page of a channel's posts
hackathon chats replies <channelId> <postId> # the replies of one thread
hackathon chats post <channelId> <your words>
hackathon chats post <channelId> --thread <postId> <your words>   # answer in a thread
```

## The jury

The jury watches each team present live and scores on paper: the application holds no jury score and no jury
ranking. On the platform a jury member reads:

- the **Teams** page: every team, with its `Demo deck`, its `Presentation` and its `Vote presentation`, the
  Slideless links the team handed in. The demo deck shows the app working in one to three episodes of real screenshots, written to be
  read alone, step by step, before or after the pitch;
- the **Vote** page's leaderboard, once the organizers have released the community result. The jury never
  casts a ballot: the community vote is the participants', and nothing combines it with the jury's scoring.

The Chats and the submissions are not open to the jury.
